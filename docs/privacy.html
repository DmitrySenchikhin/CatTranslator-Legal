Privacy Policy

Cat Companion (bundle id com.dmitrysenchikhin.catcompanion) is an on-device cat vocalization interpreter for entertainment.

What we collect

The app stores cat profiles, translation history, optional Teach AI examples, and daily free-usage counters on your iPhone using Apple frameworks (SwiftData and UserDefaults). Microphone audio is processed on the device to analyze a vocalization. We do not operate a Cat Companion cloud backend in this version.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only when you start a translation recording. Audio is analyzed locally and may be saved in the app’s private documents folder as part of your translation history.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are handled by Apple StoreKit. We receive entitlement status through Apple’s APIs, not your payment card details.

Third parties

We do not sell personal data. If analytics or crash reporting is added later, this page will be updated before release.
